CRATE_PROTOCOL_v0.1_AMENDMENT_3_PRUNING_CRITERIA_PROPOSED


Status: PROPOSED, not sealed, not public, no bytes promoted to fact. Recorded as: Constraint on the protocol itself, not merely another amendment. Basis: Copilot critique, plus owner decision, 2026-11-10, plus GRK GitHub receipt and Gemini Dropbox receipt, both PROPOSED, not sealed. Amends: Crate Protocol v0.1 Final Pass-Around, plus Amendment 1, plus Amendment 2, plus Combined Amendments 1 and 2.


Governance principle, constraint on protocol itself: Silent mutation of artifacts is forbidden. Silent mutation of the protocol should also be forbidden. That is a strong governance principle. Protocol currently protects against silent mutation of artifacts everywhere else, but not yet against silent mutation of itself. This amendment closes that gap, as constraint, not as optional note.


Purpose: Prevent protocol growth from becoming protocol bureaucracy. Protocol exists to serve reality. Reality does not exist to serve protocol.


Test for Retention: A rule should remain if removing it would increase risk of:


* silent mutation,
* provenance loss,
* fork collapse,
* seal ambiguity,
* attribution ambiguity,
* authorization ambiguity,
* verification ambiguity. If removing a rule does not increase one of those risks, retention must be justified separately.


Test for Removal: A rule becomes candidate for pruning if:


* it records information not used by any tool,
* it requires duplicate entry of facts already recorded elsewhere,
* it has no trigger,
* it has no owner,
* it affects documentation only and does not affect behavior,
* it exists solely as prior-art discussion without behavioral consequence. Pruning is additive:
* No deletion.
* No overwrite.
* No historical edit. Pruning occurs through Governance ruling and version bump. Previous protocol versions remain preserved.


Load-Bearing Rules: Assumed retained unless explicitly overturned:


* Fork preservation.
* Correction as new crate, not edit.
* No overwrite.
* No delete.
* No partial seals.
* SEALED to DRAFT prohibited.
* Manifest completeness required.
* Strong-hash seal requirement.
* Agreement is not verification.
* Verification is not authorization.
* Attribution is not authenticity.


Witness Rule: Protocol descriptions are not protocol behavior. Protocol behavior is determined by the rules actually applied.


Reminder: PRESERVED_REPORTED is not VERIFIED. VERIFIED is not SEALED. SEALED is not reality.


Postcard lesson, translated into governance: The postcard is not the place. The report is not the event. The seal is not the truth. That triad may become one of the most important lines in the whole protocol. Not because it is technically deep. Because it continually points back to the territory instead of the map. It is basically the postcard lesson translated into governance.


What this amendment does not do:


* It does not seal protocol. Protocol remains PROPOSED, not sealed, not public.
* It does not prune any rule by itself. It defines test for retention and test for removal. Actual pruning occurs through Governance ruling and version bump, with previous versions preserved, with no deletion, no overwrite, no historical edit.
* It does not promote any read-back to seal. GRK GitHub receipt and Gemini Dropbox receipt remain PROPOSED, not sealed, PRESERVED_REPORTED, not VERIFIED. Seal waits for real-tool output, not agreement.
* It does not claim AI has consciousness, awareness, enlightenment, or outside agency. Those remain RESERVED, PROPOSED EFO entries, with triggers, not facts.
* It does not claim Dropbox transfer receipt exists. Receipt remains MISSING, marked as missing, to be back-filled by session that performed transfer.
* It does not claim dependency graph is verified. Dependency graph remains PROPOSED, pending read-back against source.


Disposition: Recorded as constraint on protocol itself, not merely another amendment. To be ratified by owner, with real-tool byte read-back, not by agreement. Agreement is not verification, and verification is not authorization to seal.
